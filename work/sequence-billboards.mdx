Sequence sells billing and revenue software to finance teams. This campaign was
about brand awareness and trust: taking Sequence off the website and into the
real world.

The line came from the marketing team and the design was mine. It ran on five
boards across New York and San Francisco, one of them two screens stacked on top
of each other, each for four or six weeks.

The design puts two sides of the brand next to each other: the retro side of
finance, in the old-style button and pointer, and the gradients that stand for
its AI and agents. I generated the gradients with shaders in code and exported
them for the motion boards, and did the layout for every format, static and
moving.
