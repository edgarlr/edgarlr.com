This campaign was about taking Sequence off the website and into the real
world. The brief came from the marketing team, and it ran across New York and
San Francisco.

For the design I wanted to set two sides of the brand against each other: the
classic side of finance, in the retro-style button and pointer, and the shader
gradients that stand for innovation, AI and agents. I explored and generated the
gradients in code, and the same gradients carried both the motion and the static
boards.
