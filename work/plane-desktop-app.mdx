## The idea

Plane’s customers run distributed teams, and so did Plane. What you need most
often about the people you work with was spread across as many tools: what time
it is where they are, who sits on your team, the handful of numbers you’d
otherwise open three tabs to find. The app pulled them into one window you could
keep open.

A desktop app rather than another page: this is reference you glance at several
times a day, and a glance shouldn’t cost a tab switch and a load.

## What it held

Timezones for everyone you work with, your team at a glance, and the basic
company data underneath both.
