## The idea

Plane’s customers run distributed teams, and so did Plane. The things you need
most often about the people you work with — what time it is where they are, who
sits on your team, the handful of numbers you’d otherwise open three tabs to
find — were spread across as many tools. The app pulled them into one window you
could keep open.

A desktop app rather than another page: this is reference you glance at several
times a day, and a glance shouldn’t cost a tab switch and a load.

## What it held

Timezones for everyone you work with, your team at a glance, and the basic
company data underneath both.
