Plane is a payroll and HR platform. This is its guide to paying a distributed
team, built as a site of its own.

I was the only engineer on it. The guide runs on Sanity, and most of the work
was content modeling. Every format it uses is its own content model: dynamic
graphs, tables, images, podcasts, polls, videos, chaptered videos, quotes and
downloadable assets.
