Sequence Labs is where Sequence shares what it is experimenting with in AI and
agents: what the team is learning, and projects that may never ship. It sits
apart from the main site on purpose. Labs is an exploratory playground, and it
had to look different so that what is being tested is never mistaken for what
made it into the product.

I was given the name and that brief, a site separate from the main brand, and
did the rest: the brand and its elements, the page structure, the design and the
build.

I wanted it to feel experimental and a bit bold, a brand that is not afraid to
try things and reads as a lab the moment you see it. The shaders carry most of
that. The brand is built around them, with a Konami code hidden inside,
alongside the particles and the parallax. The control strip on the landing page
is live, so visitors can tune the shader themselves; internally I had a fuller
set of controls for fine-tuning it.
